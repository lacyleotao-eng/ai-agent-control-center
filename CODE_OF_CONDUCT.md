# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming to everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient.
- Focus on the work and explain technical disagreements clearly.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes.
- Protect other people’s privacy and confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing credentials, malware, or unsafe external actions.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository’s available GitHub moderation or security channels. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential when possible.
